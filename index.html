Four ways in

Compete

National contests with real prize money, like the Probability Challenge run with Jane Street (₹3.8 lakh prize pool). Bring a team or go solo.

Learn

Hands-on workshops in AI, machine learning, cybersecurity, IoT, robotics and the stock market, with certificates from IIT Bombay.

Listen

Lectures from scientists, space and defence leaders and Nobel-level researchers who have visited the campus in past editions.

Explore

Exhibits and demos across the campus, from robots to record attempts. Techfest has set a Guinness World Record for most LED lights lit simultaneously.
